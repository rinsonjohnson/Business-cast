A little about Forma

A good CV should be easier to make.

Forma helps you turn your experience into a clear, polished CV—even if you have never made one before. Choose a career area, follow the prompts, and watch your CV come together as you type.

01

Choose your direction

Pick IT, healthcare, business, education, creative work, hospitality, or general. The prompts help you describe the work that matters in your field.

02

Add proof of your work

Include experience, training, skills, projects, volunteering, and links such as LinkedIn, GitHub, a portfolio, or work samples. No experience yet? Start with projects or study.

03

Preview and save for free

Review the live preview, then choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog. Your details are saved in this browser on this device; Forma does not upload them.

Tip for healthcare CVs: describe your skills and responsibilities without including information that identifies a patient.
